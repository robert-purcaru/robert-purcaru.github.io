Feature selection for linear SVMs with cross-validation expressed as constraints.
We formulate the k-fold cross-validation problem as a monolithic mixed-binary quadratic program
that commercial solvers can handle, and derive bounds on the big-M constraints. On synthetic and
credit-risk data, our proposed approach outperforms other common feature-selection methods for linear SVMs,
while returning sparse models without an explicit a priori limit on the number of features.
