Data recovery for high-altitude balloon experiments. During the telescope's 2023 flight around the
southern hemisphere, science data was stored in capsules carrying 5 TB of
storage, GPS, an Iridium transmitter, and a parachute. To recover the data, the capsules were dropped over
Argentina, landed within 2.4 km of their predicted positions, and were recovered
with all data intact.
