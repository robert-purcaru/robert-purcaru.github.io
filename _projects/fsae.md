The university of Toronto Formula SAE Team designs, builds, and races a 
formula-style car each year for the Formula SAE competitions. I joined 
the team in 2020, where I worked on the last combustion car that the team 
built, and thereafter the team's first electric vehicle. I was the electrical 
lead, bringing up the car's high-voltage systems, electronics hardware, and 
control firmware. I also contributed to the manufacturing of the car through 
chasis welding and machining custom parts for the suspension system.
